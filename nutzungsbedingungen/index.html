Terms of Use – FinXorra

Last updated: 21 September 2026

1. Provider and scope

FinXorra is a self-hosted personal household bookkeeping application by Patrick Kraft (contact: Kraft.DevOps@web.de). It is not offered to the public and is used solely by the operator for their own accounts. There is no access for third parties.

2. Services

With the operator's explicit consent, the application retrieves read-only account information (accounts, balances, transactions) of the operator's own bank accounts via Enable Banking and analyses it for private household finances. No payments are initiated.

3. Consent

Access to a bank account requires approval at the bank (strong customer authentication) and lasts only for the period granted by the bank. Consent can be withdrawn at any time in the application ("Disconnect") or at the bank.

4. Liability

The application is provided without warranty for completeness or accuracy; the bank's records are authoritative. It does not constitute tax, legal or financial advice.
